Who I Am

Hey there! I'm Abinash, a third-year BSc student minoring in Data Science, with a real passion for diving into the world of machine learning and AI. I kind of stumbled into this field by accident—what began as a spark of curiosity has turned into an incredible adventure that I’m totally hooked on. My tech journey kicked off with learning the basics of Python, and before I knew it, I was geeking out over libraries like NumPy and Pandas. The game-changer for me was discovering Andrew Ng’s machine learning courses—they completely blew my mind and showed me just how powerful AI could be. That inspired me to complete the Machine Learning Specialization and Deep Learning Specialization from deeplearning.ai, and I couldn’t stop there. I dove even deeper with Andrej Karpathy’s neural networks playlist, which was like unlocking a whole new level of understanding. Over time, I’ve worked on a bunch of projects that brought my learning to life. Each one has taught me something fresh about turning ideas into real, practical solutions. Right now, I’m super excited about landing an internship where I can tackle real-world problems, put my skills to work, and soak up knowledge from seasoned pros.
